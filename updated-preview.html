Questions before your session

How do I get a quote?

Send the idea, placement, approximate size, budget comfort range and a reference image if you have one.

Is my preferred date guaranteed?

No. The form sends a request, not a confirmed appointment.

Can I send reference images?

Yes. The production booking flow accepts a reference image so Tony can see the direction or an existing tattoo.

Do you take cover-ups and reworks?

Yes. Include a clear image of the existing tattoo and explain what you want to change.

What are Tony’s rate, minimum and deposit?

Those details have not been supplied, so the site does not invent them. Tony can confirm pricing directly.
